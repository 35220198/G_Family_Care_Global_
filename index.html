Our Services

Care That Comes to You

Practical, professional care designed around the person, the home environment, and the level of support required. Each service is explained clearly so families can understand what to expect.

Personal Care

Respectful day-to-day assistance that helps clients maintain hygiene, comfort, dignity, and independence at home.

What we provide

  • Bathing and personal hygiene assistance
  • Dressing and grooming support
  • Safe mobility and transfer assistance
  • Feeding and daily-living assistance where required

Medical Care

Professional clinical support delivered with attention to safety, accurate treatment routines, and ongoing health monitoring.

What we provide

  • Medication support as directed by the care plan
  • Wound-care assistance and clinical follow-up
  • Basic health observations and monitoring
  • Communication with the appropriate clinical team

House Calls

Convenient home visits for clients who need professional health assessment, follow-up, or care without travelling to a facility.

What we provide

  • Home-based health assessments and reviews
  • Follow-up visits after illness or discharge
  • Routine monitoring and care-plan reviews
  • Referral or escalation when further medical attention is needed

Advice & Coordination

Practical guidance that helps families understand care options, organise services, and keep communication clear between the people involved in a client's care.

What we provide

  • Care planning and service coordination
  • Guidance on next steps and available care options
  • Communication with families and relevant providers
  • Follow-up to help keep care organised

Elderly Care

Personalised support for older adults focused on safety, dignity, comfort, daily routines, and maintaining independence at home.

What we provide

  • Support with daily routines and personal care
  • Mobility and safety assistance
  • Health observation and care-plan follow-up
  • Respectful care that promotes comfort and independence
